Great food, in our opinion at Tattva Bar and Café, is an adventure that entices your taste buds and leaves you wanting more. Chicken Skewers, a traditional meal that blends succulent chicken with a combination of robust spices and flavours, is one such must-try item on our menu.

Why Chicken Skewers Are the Greatest Appetiser

Chicken skewers that are expertly grilled have a certain charm. Everyone loves this dish because of the grill's smokiness, the spicy kick, and the chicken's tender texture. Every bite of our expertly seasoned chicken skewers is guaranteed to be unforgettable since they combine classic and modern flavours.

What Makes Our Skewers Unique:

  • 1.The Tattva Touch Marinade Magic To make a marinade that gives the chicken a burst of flavour, we use fragrant spices, and a touch of lemon.
  • 2.The ideal ratio of smokiness and softness is achieved by grilling each skewer until it turns golden brown.
  • 3.Delightful Dips: When served with our exclusive sauces, such as pickle mayo dip, these skewers are a taste explosion.
  • 4.The ideal pairing is to have them with one of our handcrafted cocktails or cool mocktails for a genuinely fulfilling lunch.

Perfect for Any Situation

Our Chicken Skewers are the perfect appetiser or snack whether you're having a laid-back evening, catching up with friends, or going on a casual date night. They are a great addition to your eating experience because they are meaty but light.

Come Eat with Us for a Memorable Experience

Are you prepared to indulge? Stop by Tattva Bar and Café and indulge in our delicious chicken skewers. This dish, which has been lovingly prepared and presented, is sure to make your stay unforgettable.
