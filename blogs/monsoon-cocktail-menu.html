Signature Monsoon Cocktails

Each cocktail in the monsoon menu has been designed to bring warmth, freshness, or a zesty spark — perfect for rainy evenings.

Masala Toddy

Masala chai meets brandy, lime, and triple sec in this warm, spiced citrusy drink — ideal for those cozy, drizzly nights.

Spiced Jamun Picante

A bold twist on tequila, featuring Patron Reposado, spiced jamun, coriander, lime, and chilli pepper — smoky, spicy, and unforgettable.

Tea Leaf Tempest

A vodka-based creation infused with green tea and bay leaf, balanced with amla, ginger syrup, and a hint of saline — refreshing yet sophisticated.

Mezcal Spritz

For those who love a smoky fizz — grapefruit-coriander cordial meets soda for a zesty herbal note.

Irish Expression

Old Monk rum, espresso, and a luscious tiramisu foam — a decadent cocktail that doubles as dessert in a glass.

These drinks not only warm your palate but also showcase Tattva’s commitment to creativity and quality — making it one of the most unique bars near Andheri.

How do you make a monsoon drink?

A good monsoon drink incorporates warming spices, fresh herbs, and seasonal fruits — balancing warmth and refreshment.

What is the best drink for a rainy day?

The Masala Toddy is perfect for rainy nights, while Jamun Basil Breeze offers a refreshing non-alcoholic option.

What should a cocktail menu include?

Which juice is best in monsoon?
